Siddur – Tefillah

A siddur that knows what day it is. Siddur shows the right tefillah for today — with the day’s additions, like יעלה ויבא, הלל or טל ומטר, already in place, and the lines you don’t say today left out.

Coming soon to the App Store

What it does

  • Shacharis, Mincha and Maariv in Ashkenaz, Sefard, Ari and Edot HaMizrach
  • Hallel, Musaf, Hoshanos, the Torah reading and no-Tachanun days, marked before each section
  • Zmanim for where you are
  • A Hebrew and civil calendar with candle lighting, havdalah and fast times — and export to your iPhone’s Calendar, choosing exactly what to add
  • Sefiras HaOmer with a nightly reminder
  • A compass that points to Jerusalem
  • Find or start a minyan near you

Siddur is free. No ads, no subscriptions, and nothing is locked.

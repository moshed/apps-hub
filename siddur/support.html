Siddur – Tefillah

Need help, found a mistake in the text, or have an idea? Email siddur@dancykier.com. You can also write from the app: Settings → Contact Support.

The wrong tefillah or day shows

Siddur uses your location to decide if you are in Israel and when the day ends. Check that Location is allowed in Settings → Privacy → Location Services → Siddur. If you are visiting Israel and keep two days of Yom Tov, turn that on in Siddur’s Settings.

A line I say is hidden

Siddur hides lines that are not said today. Turn on Settings → “Show hidden lines” to see them, dimmed.

Zmanim look off

Zmanim follow your current location and time zone. Make sure your iPhone’s time zone is set automatically.

Reminders don’t arrive

Open Settings → “Zmanim, Shabbos & Omer alerts” in Siddur and allow notifications when asked.

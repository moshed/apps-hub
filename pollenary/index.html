Pollenary

Pollen forecast & alerts

Most pollen apps show you one number. Pollenary shows you which plants are actually in the air — for every day of the forecast — and warns you before a bad day arrives.

Plant by plant, day by day

Ragweed on Saturday. Grass on Sunday. Tree pollen all week. Not one blended number.

One alert per allergen

Pick the ones you react to. Each gets its own notification, so a ragweed warning never buries a grass warning.

Early warning

Told days ahead, then again each morning as the bad day gets closer.

No account. Ever.

No sign-up, no email, no password. We store a ZIP code and your settings. We don't know who you are.

Where the numbers come from

Severity comes from Google's Pollen API on the Universal Pollen Index (0–5), at 1 km resolution. We checked it against real pollen-counting stations before trusting it — including NAB-reporting stations in Manhattan and Armonk — and it tracked the measured grain counts. A widely-used alternative source did not, so we don't use it for severity.
